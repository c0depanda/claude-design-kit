Halcyon · P1

Send a payment

How a member sends money to a saved or new recipient, from choosing who to the result. Every state of every step is a frame, at each width it is designed for.

Principles. The recipient's name is confirmed before any money moves. Fees and the total are shown before the person confirms. A status is said once.

Shared components. HxTextInput (44 tall), HxRadio, HxStatusBadge, HxButton primary for the one main action per screen; a bottom sheet on phones where wider screens use a popover.

  1. 1 RECIPIENT: choose who, and see the name confirmed (1.1).
  2. 2 AMOUNT: enter the amount and the speed (2.1–2.4).
  3. 3 REVIEW: check the fee and the total, then confirm (3.1–3.3).
  4. 4 RESULT: sent, processing or failed, with the reference (4.1–4.3).

Cast and sample data. Ada Example sends from her Main account ($18,480.00) to Ben Sample. The payment is $1,250.00, reference HX-PAY-1042. Long values on purpose: Bartholomew Example-Longfellow, $1,250,000.00.
